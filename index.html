THE SYSTEM

The 6-Lever System

Your Google Business Profile is the highest-leverage asset your business owns — and almost nobody manages it properly. We run all 6 levers, every week, so you're the business that shows up first.

1. Professional photos
At onboarding, we replace your photos with a set built to make your business look like the obvious choice the moment someone lands on your profile.
2. Full profile optimization & citation consistency
We run a full audit against Google's ranking factors and rebuild what's quietly costing you visibility, then track down and fix every place your business info is wrong across the web — directories most owners don't even know exist — so Google trusts your listing and keeps you ranked higher every month.
3. Weekly maintenance: posts & photo rotation
Weekly posts calibrated to what actually drives engagement in your category, paired with a steady rotation of fresh photos — the ongoing upkeep that keeps your profile looking active so you never fall behind the businesses that go quiet.
4. A review growth system built to last forever
We sit down with you to build a review growth strategy tailored to your business, then run it every week, so the plan keeps working long after we put it in place.
5. Fast review replies
The moment a review comes in, we're on it. Every review — good or bad — gets a fast, professional reply, so customers and Google see a business that's actually active and paying attention.
6. An in-store review system that makes it effortless
We create a custom QR code for your business that links straight to your review page — placed at the counter, on receipts, wherever your customers can't miss it — so leaving a review takes ten seconds instead of ten minutes.
