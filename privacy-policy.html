Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Local Growth Agency (“Local Growth Agency,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we may share it, and the choices available to you when you visit our website, submit a lead form, request a Google Business Profile audit, or otherwise communicate with us.

By using our website or submitting a form, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Your full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your clinic or business name and city.
  • Your stated marketing goals and areas of interest.
  • Your selected monthly revenue range.
  • Information you provide when requesting an audit, booking a call, or contacting us.

We may also collect certain information automatically, including:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages viewed, buttons clicked, form interactions, referral source, and approximate location.
  • Cookie, advertising, and analytics identifiers.

2. How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Review your Google Business Profile and provide the audit or information you requested.
  • Contact you by email, phone, or text regarding your audit request or a related consultation.
  • Schedule appointments and follow up with prospective clients.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website, forms, advertising, and services.
  • Measure advertising performance and understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to measure website activity, understand campaign performance, and improve advertising. These technologies may collect information about your browser, device, website activity, and interactions with our ads.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings and advertising preferences offered by the relevant platform. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as:

  • Meta, when you submit an Instant Form or interact with our advertising.
  • Formspree, when a website form is submitted.
  • Calendly, when you choose to schedule a call.
  • Website hosting, email, analytics, security, and customer-management providers.
  • Professional advisers or authorities when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not share your contact information with unrelated third parties for their own direct marketing.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, follow up on business inquiries, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information. No online system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop marketing communications. To make a request, contact us using the information below.
